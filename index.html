Why Visit Cameroon?

Nestled at the crossroads of West and Central Africa, Cameroon earns its nickname "Africa in Miniature" by bringing together every major African climate and landscape—from Saharan savannas in the Far North to lush equatorial rainforests in the South.

1. Unmatched Biodiversity

Nature Without Limits

Climb Mount Cameroon (4,095m), spot elephants and lions in Waza National Park, or watch the Lobé Waterfalls cascade directly into the Atlantic Ocean.

2. 250+ Living Cultures

A Mosaic of Traditions

From the intricate hand-embroidered Toghu regalia of the Grassfields to the coastal Ngondo water festival, every region celebrates living heritage.

3. World-Class Gastronomy

Flavors of the 237

Savor national classics like Ndolé, spicy Achu & Yellow Soup, grilled Kribi sea fish, and globally prized Penja white pepper.
